Life Without Defense

What would happen to every matchup in league history if D/ST points were removed from both sides? Six seasons, re-scored.

Methodology

For every real matchup since 2020 (regular season plus real playoff bracket games -- no consolation weeks), each team's score has its own D/ST's started points subtracted out, then the two adjusted scores are compared to see who would have actually won. If a defense scored negative points, subtracting that negative number adds the points back rather than penalizing further -- a bad week from your defense doesn't get double-counted against you. Thomas Sullivan and William Serafin (2020-only participants) are excluded, matching the rest of the site.
