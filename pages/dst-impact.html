Life Without Defense

What would happen to every matchup in league history if D/ST points were removed from both sides? Six seasons, re-scored.

Methodology

For every real matchup since 2020 (regular season plus real playoff bracket games -- no consolation weeks), each team's score has its own D/ST's started points subtracted out, then the two adjusted scores are compared to see who would have actually won. If a defense scored negative points, subtracting that negative number adds the points back rather than penalizing further -- a bad week from your defense doesn't get double-counted against you. Thomas Sullivan and William Serafin (2020-only participants) are excluded, matching the rest of the site.

Would The Playoffs Change?

Per season: who'd make the top-8 playoff field under regular-season records with defense removed, versus who actually made it. Also whether any real playoff game -- using the actual bracket matchups that happened -- would have flipped. 2020 and 2021 had non-standard playoff formats (2020's field was nearly the whole league; 2021 had a single wildcard game), so treat those two years' playoff-field comparison as a looser approximation than 2022-2025.

How Much Does Defense Actually Matter?

Same method, run for every position: remove that position's started points from both teams in all 598 games and see how often the winner changes. Puts defense's impact in context against the rest of the roster.

Does Drafting A Defense Early Actually Help?

Real PPG for every D/ST while on the drafting manager's roster, by the round it was actually drafted (D/ST has only ever been drafted rounds 8-16 in league history). Dashed line is the league-wide waiver/free-agent D/ST PPG for comparison. Rounds 8 and 16 have very small samples (8 and 5 real-game weeks) -- read those two bars cautiously.

How Predictable Is Each Position, Week To Week?

For every player-season, how much does their own week-to-week score swing relative to their own average? Taller bars mean less predictable -- even knowing how good a player usually is, you can't count on any given week matching that average.
