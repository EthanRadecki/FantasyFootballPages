Waiver Value

Position-adjusted value produced per week rostered, for every waiver and free agent pickup (2020-2025).

Methodology
  • Scoring systems changed over time: FAAB bidding only in 2025. 2020-2024 ran on reverse-order waiver priority, where a successful claim sends you to the back of the line. Since "cost" isn't comparable across those two systems, this page measures pure output instead.
  • Position-adjusted value: raw points aren't fair across positions, a mediocre QB pickup outscores a great TE pickup every week just because QBs score more. So every pickup gets scored on how many standard deviations above or below the average rostered player at that position, that week, across the whole league. A value of 0 means exactly average for a rostered player at that position.
  • Most pickups score below zero. That's expected, not a bug, since most waiver adds are below-average by definition.
  • Stints: a stint ends at the next drop or trade-away, whichever comes first. Bye weeks are kept (real production, even without a fantasy opponent that week), but consolation/loser-bracket weeks are excluded, since teams are often already eliminated and not managed seriously by that point.
  • Excluded: Thomas Sullivan and William Serafin (2020 only), same as the rest of the site.
Career Leaderboard
Points per week rostered, ranked by position-adjusted value.

The bright bar shows points per week rostered (PPW) (bottom axis), the intuitive number. The faint background bar shows number of pickups (top axis), scaled to this view, so a leaderboard built on 2 pickups looks visibly thinner than one built on 40. Managers are ranked by position-adjusted value (z-score vs. other rostered players at that position), so bar order and bar length won't always match, that's the point. The axis and color scale rescale to fit each position (QB and K don't share a range), but stay fixed across seasons so year-to-year comparisons at the same position are fair. Hover a row for the full breakdown, including that manager's best pickup in this view.

Total Value Earned by Manager
Sum of position-adjusted value across every pickup. Volume and quality together, not just average quality.

This is a different lens than the average-based leaderboards above: a manager who made many decent pickups can out-total a manager who made a few great ones. Bars can go negative since most individual pickups score below zero by definition (see methodology above). The weeks-rostered filter lets you separate pickups that actually mattered from every add ever made, including same-week drops. Click a manager's bar to see their top pickups.

Waiver Upside (Positive-Only)
Same pickups, but every add that scored below replacement is treated as zero instead of a loss. Only real hits count.

Career totals, all positions by default, no minimum weeks rostered. Bars only ever go up since bad and neutral adds contribute nothing here, unlike the total above. Click a manager's bar to see every pickup behind it.

Value by Week of the Season
Every pickup, grouped by the week it was added. When does volume peak, and when is the value actually good?

Bars (left axis) are pickup volume that week, split waiver vs. free agent. The line (right axis) is average position-adjusted value of pickups added that week, all types combined. Click a bar to see the top pickups from that week. Use the weeks-rostered filter to separate pickups that actually stuck around from same-week churn. Weeks 15–17 are playoff weeks, only teams still alive are actively managing by then, so both volume and the sample size behind that average drop off fast.
