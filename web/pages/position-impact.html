How Much Does Each Position Matter?

The same "remove it and recheck the winner" method from the defense analysis, run for every position -- QB, RB, WR, TE, K, and D/ST, side by side.

Methodology

For every real matchup since (regular season plus real playoff bracket games, no consolation weeks -- games total), each position's started points get subtracted from both teams' scores to see how often the winner would change, how predictable that position is week-to-week, and how well a team's production at that position predicts their actual win%.

Which Position Decides The Most Games?

% of all games that would have had a different winner if that position didn't exist.

Which Position Is Hardest To Predict?

Coefficient of variation: how much a player's own week-to-week score swings relative to their own average. Taller bars mean less predictable.

Which Position's Performance Predicts Winning Most?

Correlation (r) between a team's season-long production at that position and their regular-season win%, across manager-seasons.

Does Draft Round Predict Winning, For Any Position?

Correlation (r) between the round a manager's Nth pick at that position was drafted and their season win%.

Does Drafting Early Help? (Every Position)

Real PPG by the round each manager's pick at that position was drafted ( -- see methodology above). Dashed line is the league-wide waiver/free-agent PPG baseline for that position.

Does Being First To Draft It Help? (Every Position)

Every position's PPG by draft order, indexed to their own 1st pick = 100%, so shapes are directly comparable regardless of each position's raw scoring scale. A flat line near 100 means draft order doesn't matter; a line that slides downward means it does. Only well-sampled ranks (20+ real weeks) are plotted.

Exact PPG By Draft Order (Detail)

Within each season, rank managers by draft order at that position (1st team to take one, 2nd team, and so on), regardless of which round that happened in. Bars past a certain rank get thin (fewer managers draft that deep at every position every year); hover for sample size.
