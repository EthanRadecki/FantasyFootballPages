Life Without Defense

What would happen to every matchup in league history if D/ST points were removed from both sides?

Methodology

For every real matchup since (regular season plus real playoff bracket games -- no consolation weeks), each team's score has its own D/ST's started points subtracted out, then the two adjusted scores are compared to see who would have actually won. If a defense scored negative points, subtracting that negative number adds the points back rather than penalizing further -- a bad week from your defense doesn't get double-counted against you.

How Much Does Defense Actually Matter?

Same method, run for every position: remove that position's started points from both teams in all games and see how often the winner changes. Puts defense's impact in context against the rest of the roster.
